Drop multiple PDF slide decks into the queue. Slidecast extracts selectable text on your device, then reads it with your chosen device voice.

Progressive slides: nearby slides that keep the same material are compared, so only the added lines are narrated. Turn off “Skip repeated slide text” to read every slide in full. Always check the extracted text when a slide contains equations, complex layouts, or diagrams.

Voice quality: choose an enhanced or natural voice if your device offers one. Voices marked “online” may send narration text to their voice provider and need internet. Keep this page open while listening; phones can pause speech when locked or backgrounded.

Images and scans: slides without selectable text are shown but cannot be read automatically. This version does not perform OCR or describe diagrams.

Your PDF files are not uploaded or saved to a server. Refreshing clears the queue. Voice and speed preferences stay on this device.
