About Gone

Type a task and press Enter. Tap its circle or line to cross it off. Tasks leave the list 24 hours after you add them. Swipe right for the red delete button, or left for the blue edit button. Tap the revealed button to act. Editing keeps the original deadline. The weekly report keeps completed and removed totals.

Expiration is checked while the app is open and when you return. iPhone can pause websites in the background.

Install on iPhone

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on if shown, and tap Add.
